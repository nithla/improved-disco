Hi, I’m Nithila!

I've become the person the room calls on when engineering says one thing and the business wants another, because I've sat on both sides of that argument enough times to know how it goes. How something gets built and why it's worth building have always felt like the same question to me, anyway. I've never had to choose.

So I'm an engineer with an MBA. I've shipped rebrands and migrations, sites built from scratch. These days, I work in product — figuring out what gets built next, and making the case for it. Six years in now, split pretty evenly between the two. Front-end to back-end, brand to data, I've been somewhere in most of it.

From concept to execution, driving results and not just output!
